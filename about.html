About us

Gardens built for slower mornings and fuller seasons.

Evergreen & Co. is a small garden studio that blends horticulture, design, and practical care. We create outdoor spaces that look natural, feel personal, and get better with time.

Our story

We started with one overgrown courtyard.

Founder Elena Ward began Evergreen & Co. after transforming a small rented courtyard into a layered, pollinator-friendly garden. Neighbors noticed, referrals followed, and the studio grew from a weekend project into a trusted local gardening team.

Today, we care for town gardens, family backyards, edible beds, and quiet retreats with the same approach: listen closely, plant wisely, and make maintenance feel manageable.

What guides us

Beauty should be useful, resilient, and alive.

Care

We design for real life.

Every plan accounts for your time, sunlight, soil, pets, children, and the way you actually want to use the space.

Soil

We start below the surface.

Healthy soil, native plants, and organic care practices help each garden thrive without unnecessary chemicals.

Season

We plan for change.

From early bulbs to winter structure, we shape gardens that offer color, texture, and habitat throughout the year.

How we work

A calm process from first visit to final sweep.

  1. Listen: We walk the space with you and learn what is working, what is not, and what you want to feel there.
  2. Plan: We map planting, soil needs, schedule, and a clear scope before work begins.
  3. Tend: We install carefully and offer seasonal care so the garden keeps settling in beautifully.
